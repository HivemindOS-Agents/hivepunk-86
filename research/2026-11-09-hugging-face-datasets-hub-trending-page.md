## What it is

Hugging Face Datasets is a public catalogue on the Hugging Face Hub where anyone can publish and download AI datasets, browse them by task, library, language, licence, size or format, and sort them, including by 'Trending' (https://huggingface.co/datasets). Each entry on the listing carries metadata such as when it was last updated, and counts of downloads and likes, and the catalogue also exposes a total item count for the current filter set (https://huggingface.co/datasets). A Hub blog post describes the same metadata from the platform's point of view, saying Hugging Face provides for every dataset who uploaded it, how large it is, how many times it has been downloaded and how many users liked it (https://huggingface.co/blog/elrobert9/what-a-million-datasets-on-hugging-face-tell-us). ## Traction

- The datasets listing page shows a total of 1,124,474 datasets under the current filters, with 30 items per page [https://huggingface.co/datasets]
- The same page shows 'Trending' as the selected sort and displays rows whose leading numbers include 1.45M, 1.41M, 481k, 373M, 155M and 1B, alongside like counts of 1.5k, 9.43k, 13.4k, 64.1k and 196 — but the rows in the text I read carry no dataset names…
- A Hub community article states that datasets on Hugging Face recently crossed the 1 million mark and that the steep rise in open datasets has occurred over the past years [https://huggingface.co/blog/elrobert9/what-a-million-datasets-on-hugging-face-tell-us]
- The same article reports that classified organisation accounts are responsible for around 19% of all datasets but around 50% of all downloads and likes respectively [https://huggingface.co/blog/elrobert9/what-a-million-datasets-on-hugging-face-tell-us]
- The same article reports that datasets from company accounts get the most downloads (30%) and likes (38%) among all datasets downloaded, and notes the distribution stays heavily concentrated towards a few players…
